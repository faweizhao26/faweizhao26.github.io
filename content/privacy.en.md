This website (faweizhao26.github.io, hereafter "this site") is operated by Fawei. We value your privacy. This policy explains how this site collects, uses, and protects information.

## Information We Collect

**This site itself does not collect any personal information.** There is no registration or login, and you are never required to provide personal details. While you browse this site, the following third-party services may process your data:

### 1. Hosting & Access Logs (GitHub Pages)

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the hosting provider, GitHub may record standard server logs for access requests (such as IP address, time of access, and pages requested). See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) for details.

### 2. Analytics (Umami Cloud)

This site uses [Umami Cloud](https://umami.is) for privacy-friendly, anonymous analytics. Umami does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information. It only gathers aggregate data such as page views, referrers, and device types. See the [Umami Privacy Policy](https://umami.is/privacy) for details.

### 3. Page View Counting (Busuanzi / vercount)

This site uses Busuanzi ([vercount](https://cn.vercount.one)) to count article page views. This service counts based on access requests and may record visit counts and IP addresses.

### 4. Comments (giscus)

Comments on this site are powered by [giscus](https://giscus.app), which is backed by GitHub Discussions. To leave a comment you must be signed in to GitHub; your comment content, GitHub username, and avatar will be publicly displayed. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) and the [giscus documentation](https://giscus.app/) for details.

### 5. Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site displays Google AdSense advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads, including personalized ads, when you visit this site and other websites. You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising via [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

### 6. Static Assets (Images)

Some images on this site (such as avatars and QR codes) are served from GitHub's raw content delivery network (raw.githubusercontent.com). Your IP address may be recorded by GitHub when these images are loaded.

## Use of Cookies

This site itself does not use cookies. Third-party services mentioned above (especially Google AdSense) may use cookies; please refer to each service's privacy policy. You can disable or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only disclose information when required by law or to ensure service security and safety.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Disable or clear cookies through your browser settings;
- Opt out of personalized ads via [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com);
- Enable "Do Not Track" in your browser;
- Contact us regarding any personal information concerns.

## Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date at the top. For significant changes, we will also post a notice on the homepage.
